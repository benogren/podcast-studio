# Assets

Optional theme music for `scripts/podcast_audio.py`. Drop in `theme.mp3` (used for both intro
and outro) or `intro.mp3` / `outro.mp3`. The intro plays right after the cold open and the
outro after the sign-off. With no files here the episode is voice-only.

Only use music you have the right to publish with. Record where each file came from and its
license in the table below.

## Where to get an intro

Search for "podcast intro" or "podcast hook" tracks (a 10-15 second sting works best):

- Epidemic Sound: https://www.epidemicsound.com/music/themes/podcast/podcast-hooks/
- Envato Elements: https://elements.envato.com/royalty-free-music/podcast+intro+music

Both are subscription services. Before you publish, confirm your plan's license covers podcast
distribution (some require registering the show or channel), and keep the license record with the
file. Download as MP3 and save it here as `theme.mp3`.
